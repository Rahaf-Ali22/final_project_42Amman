ReDoS

Regular Expression Denial of Service

What is ReDoS?

CWE-1333

ReDoS occurs when a vulnerable regular expression takes excessive time to process specially crafted input.

Attackers exploit catastrophic backtracking to consume CPU resources, slow an application, and potentially make a service unavailable.

Why it matters

      
A slow username check can tie up server resources. Repeated requests may delay sign-ins and other banking services. In 2019, Cloudflare reported that a poorly performing regular expression in a WAF rule caused a major service disruption.

      
The fix

      
Replace nested repetition with a single character class: /^[a-zA-Z]+$/. Switch to Fixed and run the same attack: the invalid username is rejected without catastrophic backtracking.
